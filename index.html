01 — Our Story Why we make matching sets
"Some of the sweetest memories happen after the lights go down — story time, giggles, matching pyjamas."

Muse Fashjewels by AYK began with a love for Korean loungewear — soft pastel tones, gentle ribbed cottons, and prints that feel calm rather than loud. We design for women and baby girls side by side, so a mother and daughter can twin in something soft on the nights that call for it.

Every set is cut for real sleep: breathable fabric, relaxed fits, and details gentle enough for a baby's skin and easy enough for a busy mother's laundry pile.

02 — Fabric & Care

Gentle enough for baby skin.

Every Muse Fashjewels set is cut from soft-washed, breathable ribbed cotton and cotton-blend fabric — pre-shrunk and finished without harsh dyes, so it's gentle against a baby's skin and comfortable for everyday wear.

Baby sets use snap closures for quick changes, women's sets are sized true-to-fit for a relaxed sleep, and every mother-daughter combo is tested to wash and wear alike, set after set.
